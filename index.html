Juan Carlos Franco Hernández

Music producer · Audio engineer · Audio post · Perceptual evaluation

Sixteen years recording, editing, mixing, mastering and producing music for artists and cultural institutions in the UK and Colombia. Sound design, Foley and voice-over sessions cut to picture, delivered to broadcast loudness specifications.

I also design and run the formal listening experiments that establish how audio is actually perceived — trained panels, written attribute scales, statistical analysis — work published in the Journal of the Audio Engineering Society, where I now review.

Listening experiments and rubric design

2019–2026
Experiment design Panel training Statistics

Doctoral work at the Institute of Sound Recording, University of Surrey, submitted October 2026: stimulus preparation, screening and training listener panels, writing the attribute definitions and scales they judged against, and non-parametric analysis to separate real perceptual differences from noise.
